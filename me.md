Xinnan is a young creator working at the intersection of design research, contemporary art, and technology. Her practice focuses on exploring relational structures through which non-human agents—such as ecology and technology—co-construct shared worlds with humans in contemporary contexts.

Xinnan’s work examines how humans re-understand nature, perception, and forms of symbiosis in a technologically saturated condition. Grounded in design research methodologies, she integrates artificial intelligence, sensing technologies, biomaterials, and narrative media to construct experimental works that combine poetic expression with critical inquiry. Through installations, moving image, data visualization, and generative systems, she investigates perceptual imbalances produced by information overload, environmental change, and anthropocentrism, while proposing future-oriented, cross-species modes of perception and collaboration. Her projects often exist as continuously evolving systems rather than static objects, emphasizing process, circulation, and regeneration.

Xinnan is currently engaged in design research at the Hybrid Ecology Lab (HY-Lab), ShanghaiTech University. Her research and creative work have been published in academic journals and exhibited internationally at platforms including Milan Design Week, Dutch Design Week, and Ithra Tanween Design Week (Saudi Arabia). Her project *RainWeaver* received the Isola Design Award (Innovation Category), among other recognitions. Spanning art, design, and science, her practice positions design as a mode of knowing and a means of reconfiguring relational structures in contemporary society.
